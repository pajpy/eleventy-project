# Dinosaur Predators

Predatory dinosaurs were meat-eating animals that hunted or scavenged for food. Many of them had sharp teeth, strong legs, and powerful senses that helped them survive.

One of the most famous predators was the **Tyrannosaurus rex**, known for its massive skull, strong bite force, and large size.

![Tyrannosaurus Rex](/images/trex.jpg)

## Common Features

- Sharp teeth
- Strong jaws
- Clawed hands or feet
- Good movement and hunting ability
- Meat-based diet

## Famous Dinosaur Predators

- Tyrannosaurus rex
- Velociraptor
- Allosaurus
- Spinosaurus

Predators played an important role in prehistoric ecosystems because they helped control herbivore populations.
